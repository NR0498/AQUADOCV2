# Model card: water-quality-mlp-ensemble-v1

## Purpose

Estimate optically active water-quality parameters for each water pixel of a Sentinel-2 Level-2A scene. Outputs are model estimates with calibrated intervals, not measurements. The model cannot detect non-optical pollutants (heavy metals, bacteria, dissolved nutrients, pesticides); those require laboratory analysis.

## Inputs

- Sentinel-2 remote-sensing reflectance Rrs (sr^-1) = L2A surface reflectance / pi
- Bands: B2, B3, B4, B5, B6, B7
- Feature version `s2-rrs-b2b7-v1`: `log10_B2`, `log10_B3`, `log10_B4`, `log10_B5`, `log10_B6`, `log10_B7`, `nd_B3_B2`, `nd_B5_B4`, `nd_B6_B4`

## Training data

- GLORIA (Lehmann et al. 2023, Sci. Data 10:100), doi:10.1594/PANGAEA.948492, CC-BY-4.0
- 7572 spectra; 615 removed by GLORIA QC flags; 1196 removed for incomplete B2–B7 coverage; **5761 used** from 44 datasets in 745 spatial groups.
- Hyperspectral Rrs convolved with ESA Sentinel-2 v4.0 spectral response functions (mean of S2A/S2B/S2C).
- Training table SHA-256 `d0eb45d4fd247905323bf580101c37a9567c7293bc9c41768c3a75fbc35e97ef`.

## Method

- Per target: ensemble of 10 MLPs (64, 64), ReLU, L2 = 0.001, trained on log10(target) with bootstrap resampling.
- Augmentation: 3 noisy copies per spectrum, 5% spectrally flat gain error and 0.0005 sr⁻¹ per-band offset, to approximate satellite radiometric and atmospheric-correction error.
- Splits by ~28 km spatial cells: 20% of groups held out for testing, 15% of the remaining groups for interval calibration.
- 80% intervals from normalized split-conformal prediction on the calibration groups.
- Deployment rule, fixed before training: a target ships only if its test MdSA beats both the train-median dummy and the published algorithm (when one exists).

## Known limitations

- **Domain gap.** Training spectra are in-situ radiometer measurements; the deployed model receives satellite reflectance after Sen2Cor atmospheric correction, which was designed for land. The held-out test scores are therefore an upper bound; the satellite validation section measures the real gap, which the residual correction narrows but does not close (worst for CDOM, which relies on the blue bands).
- **Match-up coverage.** Satellite validation is dominated by lakes; rivers and estuaries are barely represented, so their satellite accuracy is unknown.
- **Spatial representativeness.** GLORIA over-represents some regions and water types; performance elsewhere is not guaranteed.
- **Adjacency and bottom effects.** Near-shore pixels, shallow clear water and floating vegetation violate the model's optically-deep open-water assumption. The pipeline masks shorelines, but cannot detect every case.
- **Out-of-domain inputs** (features outside the training 1st–99th percentile) are flagged per pixel.
- **Interval coverage** is measured on held-out groups; values below the nominal level mean intervals are too narrow for new regions.
- Turbidity training labels are NTU from mixed instruments; Sentinel-2-based turbidity is often reported in FNU.
